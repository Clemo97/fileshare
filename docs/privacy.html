Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Acornet is a free app that sends files directly from one of your devices to another. It is made by Clement Lumumba, an individual developer, as a personal project. This page explains what information the app handles and how you can delete it.

The short version

  • There is no sign-up. Acornet never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your files are not uploaded to a server. They travel directly between your two devices, encrypted.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
  • You can delete your account and everything linked to it inside the app.

What the app handles

The app keeps a small amount of information on a server so that two devices can find each other. It is stored with Supabase, a hosting service, in a data centre in Frankfurt, Germany.

InformationWhyHow long
An anonymous account. A random ID that the app creates when you first open it. A sign-in token is kept on your device. So the app can recognise your device and pair it with another. Until you delete the account.
A random name, a colour and an animal. Shown on the device you pair with, so you can tell your devices apart. Until you delete the account.
Pairing codes and pairings. The short code you generate, and a record of which two accounts are paired. Failed code attempts are counted to stop people guessing codes. To pair two devices safely. Codes last a few minutes. Pairings and the attempt counts last until the pairing or the account is deleted.
Transfer requests. The names, sizes and types of the files you offer to send. So the other device can ask whether you want to accept. Removed when the transfer ends. If the app is closed halfway, they can stay until you delete your account.
Connection messages. Technical messages that set up the direct link between your devices. They contain the network (IP) addresses of the devices. To connect the two devices directly. Removed when the connection is made. If the app is closed halfway, they can stay until you delete your account.

Your files

The content of your files is never stored on a server. It is sent directly from one device to the other over an encrypted connection. A file you receive is first written to the app's temporary storage on your device, and the app then opens the system share sheet so you can save or open it. The system may clear temporary storage when space runs low, so save a file once it arrives.

The app can only read files that you choose in the system file picker.

Other services involved

  • Supabase hosts the account, pairing and transfer-setup information described above and, like any web service, can see the IP address of each request. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • STUN servers run by Cloudflare and Google. When two devices connect, each asks a STUN server to tell it its public network address. These servers see that address. See the Cloudflare and Google privacy policies.

Acornet does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not share information with anyone else.

Permissions

  • Local network. iOS may ask whether Acornet can find devices on your local network. It uses this to connect directly to your other device on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Camera and microphone. The app never uses them. Their permission texts exist only because the networking library the app uses is able to capture video and audio.
  • The app does not use your location, contacts or photo library.

Deleting your data

Open the app, go to the home screen, tap Delete account and confirm. This removes your account and the pairings, codes, transfer requests and connection messages linked to it. The app then starts again with a new anonymous account.

It does not remove files you already saved to your devices, because those are on your devices, not on a server. Hosting providers may keep their own short-lived technical logs under their own policies.

Security

The app talks to the server over HTTPS, and files travel between devices over an encrypted connection (DTLS, part of WebRTC). No system is perfectly secure, but your files do not sit on a server where they could be leaked.

Children

Acornet is not aimed at children. It does not ask for personal information such as a name or email address from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, I will update this page and the date at the top.
